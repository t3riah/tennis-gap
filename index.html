Built from hand-charted matches in Jeff Sackmann's Match Charting Project, plus — for the most recent matches — point-by-point tapes from the Live Tennis API, folded in only where a match's serve data passes a coverage check. Recent matches count more, and small samples are pulled toward tour averages. Surface-specific numbers appear after five charted matches on that surface; before that, the all-surface profile is used and labelled. The serve model behind the win probability is calculated exactly from first-serve, second-serve, return and double-fault splits — no simulation noise — and the headline percentage blends it with surface Elo, calibrated on a walk-forward backtest of 5,186 tour matches. For the men, profiles for in-form players come from official ATP match stats instead of charted matches (through-date in the freshness line above). Break-point and hold-point counts are recency-weighted toward recent matches, so the saved/converted fractions are rounded. Serve placement is read from charted matches for every player, including men whose other numbers come from official stats — the official feed doesn't record serve direction. Stats are informational only, not betting advice.
